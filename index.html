Choose a countryHow it works

Small businesses hold up the economy. Conflict leaves them without customers.

In conflict-affected countries, small business owners make the food, crafts and goods that keep local economies moving. But poverty, instability and broken supply routes cut them off from buyers beyond their own borders. They keep working, and the orders don't come.

Give them a market in the West.

Hand to Hand connects small business owners with customers abroad through a monthly subscription. Instead of one-off sales, makers get repeat orders from people who chose their work. We buy directly from them, at prices we agree on in writing.

Not a donation. An order.

Your box is a real purchase of real products, made by people who are glad to have a customer.
