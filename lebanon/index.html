Made to Taste.
Made to Keep.

From traditional foods and spices to handmade ceramics, soaps, and other goods, our first collection is exploring products created by Lebanese businesses. Each item represents an opportunity to share their work with customers beyond their local markets.

Examples of the kinds of products makers in Lebanon create. Your box will be different.

  • Za'atar Blend

    A herb and sesame blend used on bread, eggs and labneh. The mix varies by recipe.

  • Olive oil Soap

    A solid soap made using olive oil. Shapes, textures and formulations vary.

  • Pistachio Sweets

    Pistachio sweets come in many forms, including filled pastries and small confections.

  • Hand-painted Ceramic dish

    A ceramic dish decorated with painted patterns. Forms, finishes and decorative techniques vary.

  • Sumac Spice blend

    A tangy spice blend based on ground sumac, used as a seasoning or finishing touch.

  • Beaded Bracelet

    An accessory made by arranging beads along a thread or cord. Bead shapes, patterns and materials vary.
